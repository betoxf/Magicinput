# Licensing and pricing

MagicInput is free for personal use. Companies pay per device.

| | Who | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Personal** | Your own computers at home, hobby and personal projects, students, schools, charities, public research and government organizations | Free |
| **Business** | Companies, and anyone using MagicInput for commercial work | **$10 per device per month** |

A device is each computer that runs MagicInput. A Mac and a PC sharing one keyboard are two devices.

**[Buy a Business license](https://buy.stripe.com/8x29AUdj3cmq9eoe29d3i01)** and choose the number of devices at checkout. Your receipt is your proof of license. To add or remove devices, or to cancel, reply to your receipt email.

## The terms

- The code is published under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). It allows any noncommercial use: personal use and use by noncommercial organizations such as schools, charities, public research and government.
- A Business license lets your organization use MagicInput for any purpose, including commercial use, on the number of devices you pay for, while the subscription is active. Otherwise the PolyForm terms apply, including the limits on liability.
- MagicInput doesn't check licenses or phone home. Licensing works on trust.
