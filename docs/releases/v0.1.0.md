The first public release of **MagicInput**: one keyboard and mouse for your Mac and your Windows PC.

### Highlights

- Push the pointer off the edge of one screen and keep going on the other. Works in both directions.
- **Auto** mode needs no setup; or drag the PC next to your displays (iPad included) for an exact fit.
- Copied text follows the pointer. ⌘C copies on Windows.
- Finds the other computer on your network, pairs with a short code, encrypts everything, and refuses anything outside your local network. No account, no cloud.
- A small frosted-glass panel drops from the menu bar icon (optional).

### Install

- **macOS 13+** (Apple silicon and Intel, notarized): `brew install --cask betoxf/tap/magicinput`, or download `MagicInput-macOS.zip`, unzip and move MagicInput to Applications.
- **Windows 10/11**: download `MagicInput-Windows.zip`, unzip, open `magicinput-app.exe` and accept the administrator prompt.

Then open MagicInput on both, allow Accessibility and Input Monitoring on the Mac, and pair with the link icon. See the [README](https://github.com/betoxf/Magicinput#quick-start).
