Use one keyboard and mouse across your Mac and Windows PC. **MagicInput 0.6.2 makes the screens easier to tell apart in the panel.**

### What changed

- **Every screen looks like what it is.** The other computer used to be a black box. Now a PC shows a blue Windows-style desktop with a taskbar, and a Mac shows a Mac desktop with a menu bar and a Dock. Its name sits small at the bottom.
- The Mac's main screen shows its Dock, and on the PC the panel draws its own screens in the Windows style.
- Everything from 0.6.0 and 0.6.1 is still here: **AirDrop to your Windows PC**, each keyboard staying on its own computer, and the new icon.

0.6.2 connects to computers on 0.6.x, 0.5.0 and 0.4.0. Update both computers to use AirDrop to the PC.

### Install

- **Mac (macOS 13+):** open `MagicInput.dmg` and drag MagicInput to Applications, or `brew install --cask betoxf/tap/magicinput` (`brew upgrade --cask magicinput` to update).
- **Windows 10/11:** run `MagicInput-Setup.exe` and click Install. Running a newer setup updates MagicInput and keeps your settings. The setup isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may ask you to choose **More info → Run anyway**.
