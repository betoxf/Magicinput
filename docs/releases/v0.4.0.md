Use one keyboard and mouse across your Mac and Windows PC. **MagicInput 0.4.0** adds a free trial and Premium, where one license covers all your computers.

### New

- **14-day free trial** on every computer, no card needed.
- **One license for your setup.** After the trial, MagicInput keeps sharing as long as one of your computers has Premium: it unlocks the computer connected to it too. Plans: $10 a month, $96 a year or $199 once, at [bullfighterstudio.com/magicinput](https://bullfighterstudio.com/magicinput).
- **Activate in the panel:** click **Free trial** at the bottom of the panel and paste the license key from your download page (or run `magicinput license <KEY>`). Keys are checked on your computer; monthly and yearly keys renew themselves once a day.
- `magicinput status` shows Premium and the trial.
- Microsoft Store version (coming soon): the Store purchase counts as Premium.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Update both computers.** 0.4.0 uses protocol 5, so it won't connect to 0.3.x.

### Get it

- **Mac:** `brew install --cask betoxf/tap/magicinput` (or `brew upgrade --cask magicinput`).
- **Installers and Premium:** [bullfighterstudio.com/magicinput](https://bullfighterstudio.com/magicinput): the Mac disk image and the Windows setup are on your download page with your license key.
- **Build it yourself:** [from source](https://github.com/betoxf/Magicinput#build-from-source) (GPL-3.0).

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of the disk image, the setup and the Homebrew zip. The Windows setup isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may ask you to choose **More info → Run anyway**.
